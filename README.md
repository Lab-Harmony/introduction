# LabHarmony

LabHarmony is a cloud laboratory platform for geotechnical and materials testing. Teams manage projects, boreholes, and samples; track chain of custody; assign and run experiments; review conditions and results; and collaborate across organizations with role-based access—from sample intake through completed, auditable test outcomes.

## What LabHarmony does

- Organize lab work by **project**, with boreholes, samples, and related organizations
- Track **sample lifecycle** and **chain of custody**
- Assign **experiments/tests** to samples and move them through draft, conduction, review, and completion
- Support multi-organization collaboration with **role-based permissions**
- Keep laboratory workflows auditable from intake to final results

## Who it is for

Geotechnical laboratories, materials testing labs, and project teams that need structured sample handling, test assignment, review workflows, and cross-organization coordination.
